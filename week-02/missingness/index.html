03 / EXPLANATION, TERM & FAILURE MODE

A number can replace a gap without filling it

A recorded zero says that an event's recorded duration is zero. A blank says its duration is unknown. Adding zero changes neither the sum nor the evidence about that event. It can still change how many cells a later calculation treats as measured.

Technical terms: missingness is the absence of a recorded value. Imputation assigns a replacement to a missing value; it needs a defensible rule and a record. Reconciliation accounts for each received row in the retained or removed set.

Failure mode: “The sum is unchanged, so the cleaning is safe.” That check misses altered meaning and removed events. Compare row identities, missingness and the change log as well.

Isomo connection

In a synthetic activity export, a reviewer needs to distinguish no recorded minutes from no duration measurement. Preserve the original and ask the source owner before assigning a meaning to a gap. These toy events are not learner records or an actual Isomo schema.
